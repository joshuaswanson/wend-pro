How to play

Find every hidden word.

  • Drag across letters that touch horizontally or vertically to spell a word. You can also tap letters one by one, then tap the last letter again to submit.
  • Every letter belongs to exactly one hidden word. Blank squares are never used.
  • The boxes under the board show how many words are hidden and how long each one is.
  • Hint reveals the next letter of a word. It recharges for 30 seconds after you start and after each use. Undo takes back your last word. Double tap a found word to release it.
  • Mixed hides equal numbers of English, French and German words. Each box is tagged with its language.
  • Cursed mode adds portals and fog. Two tiles with matching swirling rings are linked, so a trail can jump from one to the other. Drag straight from a portal to its twin, or tap one and then the other. Fogged tiles stay hidden until a word next to them is found.
  • Keys: H hint, U undo, N new puzzle, Esc clear trail.
